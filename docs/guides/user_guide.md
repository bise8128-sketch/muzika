# 📘 Muzika Karaoke - User Guide

Welcome to **Muzika Karaoke**, the next-generation AI-powered karaoke application! This guide will help you get started, separate audio tracks, and enjoy your favorite songs.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari)
- An internet connection (for initial model download)
- Audio files (MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A)

### Launching the App

1. Open the application in your browser.
2. Allow the application to initialize. On the first run, it may download AI models (approx. 150-300MB). This is a one-time process.

---

## 🎵 Features

### 1. 📤 Upload & Separation

Transform any song into a karaoke track!

1. **Drag & Drop** your audio file into the upload zone, or click to select a file.
2. The app will automatically analyze the file.
3. Click **"Separate Audio"** to begin the AI process.
   - *Note: This process runs entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your device!*
4. Wait for the process to complete. You'll see a progress bar indicating the status.

### 2. 🎤 Karaoke Mode

Once separated, enter the immersive Karaoke experience!

- **Vocals Control**: Use the slider to reduce or mute the original vocals.
- **Lyrics**: If lyrics are available/synced, they will appear on screen.
- **Pitch & Tempo**: Adjust the key and speed to match your singing style.
- **Visualizer**: Enjoy the dynamic audio visualizer that reacts to the music.

### 3. 🎛️ Stem Isolation (Advanced)

Want more control? Use the Mixer!

- Adjust individual volumes for:
  - **Vocals**
  - **Drums**
  - **Bass**
  - **Other Instruments**
- Solo or Mute specific tracks to practice specific parts.

### 4. 💾 Export & Save

Take your tracks with you!

- **Download Instrumental**: Save the track without vocals.
- **Download Vocals**: Save only the vocal track.
- **Export Mix**: Save your custom mix (adjusted levels).
- **Format**: High-quality WAV or MP3.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: **Yes!** Muzika runs ensuring privacy-first processing. Your audio files are processed locally on your device using WebAssembly technology.

**Q: Why does the first load take a while?**
A: The application needs to download the AI models to your browser's storage. After the first time, the app will load instantly and work offline!

**Q: What audio formats are supported?**
A: We support most common formats: MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A/AAC, and OGG.

**Q: Can I use this on mobile?**
A: Yes! Muzika is designed to be responsive. Performance may vary depending on your device's processing power.

---

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

- **Separation is slow**: Try closing other tabs or applications to free up system resources.
- **No sound**: Check your device volume and ensure the browser tab is not muted.
- **"Out of Memory" error**: Try processing a shorter audio file or using a desktop browser.
